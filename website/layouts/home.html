New kubectl joulie, a live power dashboard

Joulie: energy-aware Kubernetes

Per-node digital twins model your cluster's power and cooling in real time. Joulie caps CPUs and GPUs where it is safe and steers new pods to the nodes that can take them, so racks stay inside their power and cooling budget.

Get started Read the docs GitHub
kubectl joulie

Why it matters

As AI and scientific workloads scale, the limits of a cluster are physical before they are logical.

Cooling bottlenecks

GPU-dense racks exceed their cooling capacity during training bursts.

Power overcommit

Peak draw exceeds the PSU and PDU budget of a rack.

Energy and carbon cost

Flat power profiles waste energy when demand is low.

What Joulie does

It makes the scheduler and the controller aware of the physical energy state of every node.

A digital twin per node

A NodeTwin tracks power, headroom, predicted cooling stress and trend from live telemetry.

CPU and GPU power caps

The node agent enforces caps through RAPL and NVML and, when enabled, CPU governor presets.

Energy-aware scheduling

A scheduler extender keeps performance pods on uncapped nodes and steers the rest toward eco nodes.

Telemetry you already have

OpenTelemetry hw.power and hw.energy or Kepler counters from Prometheus, plus cAdvisor.

kubectl joulie

Cluster status, a node in detail and a live dashboard, read from the twins.

A simulator for experiments

Try policies on KWOK or emulated bare-metal nodes on one machine, before real hardware.

How it works

A closed loop between the hardware and the scheduler keeps the power envelope of the cluster stable.

Up in two commands

Install the chart from the OCI registry, then look at your nodes. No hardware at hand? The tutorials start on a simulated cluster that runs the whole stack on one machine with Docker.

# Controller manager, node agent and scheduler extender
$ helm upgrade --install joulie oci://registry.cern.ch/joulie/joulie \
    --version <version> -n joulie-system --create-namespace \
    -f values/joulie.yaml

# Live dashboard: power, caps and headroom of every node
$ kubectl joulie top
